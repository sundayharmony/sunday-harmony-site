## §609 — Disclosures to Consumers (15 U.S.C. §1681g)

Every consumer reporting agency shall, upon request, clearly and accurately disclose to the consumer:

- All information in the consumer's file at the time of the request
- The sources of the information
- Identification of each person that procured a consumer report (1 year for general purposes; 2 years for employment)
- Dates, original payees, and amounts of any checks underlying adverse characterizations
- A record of inquiries received in the prior year that identified the consumer for credit/insurance transactions not initiated by the consumer

Agencies must provide the Bureau's Summary of Rights with each written disclosure, including the consumer's right to dispute under §611.

## §611 — Procedure in Case of Disputed Accuracy (15 U.S.C. §1681i)

### Bureau reinvestigation (consumer disputes to CRA)

When a consumer disputes completeness or accuracy of an item in their file:

1. **30-day reinvestigation** — CRA must conduct a reasonable reinvestigation free of charge and record current status or delete inaccurate items before the end of 30 days from receipt of dispute notice.
2. **15-day extension** — May extend up to 15 additional days if consumer submits relevant new information during the 30-day period (with limitations).
3. **Notify furnisher within 5 business days** — CRA must notify the furnisher of the dispute with all relevant information.
4. **Consider consumer information** — CRA must review all relevant information submitted by the consumer.
5. **Delete or modify inaccurate/unverifiable information** — Promptly delete or modify; notify furnisher. Reinsertion requires furnisher certification of accuracy plus 5-day consumer notice.
6. **Results notice within 5 business days** — Written notice of results, revised consumer report, and rights to add a statement of dispute.
7. **Statement of dispute (§611(b))** — If unresolved, consumer may file a brief statement (up to 100 words with agency assistance); must appear in subsequent reports.
8. **Frivolous disputes** — CRA may terminate if dispute is frivolous or irrelevant; must notify consumer within 5 business days with reasons and required information.

### Expedited resolution

If disputed information is deleted within 3 business days, certain notice requirements may be satisfied by telephone plus written confirmation.

## §615 — Requirements on Users of Consumer Reports (15 U.S.C. §1681m)

When a person takes **adverse action** based in whole or in part on a consumer report, they must:

1. Provide notice of the adverse action
2. Disclose the credit score used (if applicable)
3. Provide the CRA name, address, and toll-free number
4. State that the CRA did not make the decision and cannot provide specific reasons
5. Inform the consumer of the right to a free report (60-day window under §612) and the right to dispute under §611

## §623 — Responsibilities of Furnishers (15 U.S.C. §1681s-2)

### Accuracy duties (§623(a))

- Do not furnish information known or reasonably believed to be inaccurate
- Do not furnish information after consumer notice at furnisher's designated address that specific information is inaccurate (when in fact inaccurate)
- Promptly correct and update information determined incomplete or inaccurate

### Direct dispute to furnisher (§623(a)(8))

Consumer direct dispute notice must:

- Identify specific disputed information
- Explain basis for dispute
- Include supporting documentation as required

Furnisher must then:

- Investigate the dispute
- Review all relevant consumer information
- Complete investigation and report results to consumer before expiration of the §611(a)(1) period
- If inaccurate, promptly notify each CRA and provide corrections

### CRA-notified dispute (§623(b))

After receiving notice from a CRA under §611(a)(2), furnisher must:

- Investigate
- Review relevant information from the CRA
- Report results to the CRA
- If incomplete or inaccurate, report to all nationwide CRAs
- Not report disputed information without noting the dispute
