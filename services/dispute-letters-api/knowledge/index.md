# Dispute-Type Index

Human-readable routing guide from letter scenario to applicable statutes. See [citations.yaml](citations.yaml) for structured lookup.

---

## Credit Bureau Dispute

**When:** Consumer disputes inaccurate or incomplete information on a credit report with Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion (or another CRA).

| Citation | Duty |
|----------|------|
| FCRA §611 (15 U.S.C. §1681i) | 30-day free reinvestigation; delete/modify unverifiable items |
| FCRA §609 (15 U.S.C. §1681g) | Right to file disclosure and Summary of Rights |
| FCRA §611(b) | Statement of dispute if unresolved |

**Key files:** [fcra/key-sections.md](fcra/key-sections.md) · [fcra/ftc-fcra-march-2026.md](fcra/ftc-fcra-march-2026.md)

## Furnisher / Creditor Dispute (Credit Reporting)

**When:** Consumer disputes directly with the entity that furnished information to a CRA (bank, collection agency furnisher, etc.).

| Citation | Duty |
|----------|------|
| FCRA §623(a)(8) (15 U.S.C. §1681s-2) | Direct dispute investigation and CRA correction |
| FCRA §623(b) (15 U.S.C. §1681s-2) | Investigation after CRA forwards dispute |
| FCRA §623(a)(1) | Prohibition on furnishing known inaccurate information |

**Key files:** [fcra/key-sections.md](fcra/key-sections.md) · [fcra/ftc-fcra-march-2026.md](fcra/ftc-fcra-march-2026.md)

## Debt Validation / Collection Dispute

**When:** Third-party debt collector contacts consumer about a personal, family, or household debt.

| Citation | Duty |
|----------|------|
| FDCPA §809 (15 U.S.C. §1692g) | 30-day validation window; cease collection until verified |
| FDCPA §805(c) (15 U.S.C. §1692c) | Cease communication on written request |
| FDCPA §§806–808 (15 U.S.C. §§1692d–1692f) | Harassment, misrepresentation, unfair practices |

**Key files:** [fdcpa/key-sections.md](fdcpa/key-sections.md) · [fdcpa/ftc-fdcpa.md](fdcpa/ftc-fdcpa.md)

---

## Credit Card / Billing Error Dispute

**When:** Consumer disputes a charge or billing error on an open-end credit account (credit card, revolving line).

| Citation | Duty |
|----------|------|
| 15 U.S.C. §1666 | 60-day written notice; creditor acknowledgment and investigation |
| 15 U.S.C. §1666(a) | 30-day acknowledgment; resolve within 2 billing cycles / 90 days |
| 15 U.S.C. §1666(c) | No collection action on disputed amount during investigation |
| 15 U.S.C. §1666a | Restrictions on adverse credit reporting during dispute |

**Key files:** [usc/15-usc-ch41-subch-i-part-d-fcba.md](usc/15-usc-ch41-subch-i-part-d-fcba.md)

## Adverse Action / Credit Denial

**When:** Consumer was denied credit, insurance, or employment based on a consumer report.

| Citation | Duty |
|----------|------|
| FCRA §615 (15 U.S.C. §1681m) | Adverse action notice with CRA contact and dispute rights |
| ECOA §1691(d) (15 U.S.C. §1691d) | Adverse action statement of reasons within 30 days |

**Key files:** [fcra/key-sections.md](fcra/key-sections.md) · [usc/15-usc-ch41-subch-iv-ecoa.md](usc/15-usc-ch41-subch-iv-ecoa.md)

## ECOA Discrimination

**When:** Consumer alleges credit discrimination based on protected characteristics.

| Citation | Scope |
|----------|-------|
| ECOA §1691 (15 U.S.C. §1691) | Prohibited discrimination in credit transactions |
| ECOA §1691(d) (15 U.S.C. §1691d) | Adverse action notice and statement of reasons |

## TILA Disclosure Framework

**When:** Letter references disclosure obligations, open-end credit terms, or billing rights under Truth in Lending.

| Citation | Scope |
|----------|-------|
| 15 U.S.C. §§1601–1665 | TILA general disclosure requirements |
| 15 U.S.C. §§1666–1666j | FCBA billing dispute procedures (Part D) |
