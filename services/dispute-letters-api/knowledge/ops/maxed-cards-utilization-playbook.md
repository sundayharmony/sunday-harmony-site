## 1. Deep summary

### Goal
When cards are maxed, treat it as a **structure / reporting** problem—not only a “pay everything tomorrow” problem. Move or reshape revolving debt so **personal utilization** drops, score recovers, and cheaper funding unlocks.

### Why it matters
- Utilization ≈ **30%** of FICO (second to payment history 35%).
- On-time payers still get crushed by high revolving use.
- “Under 30%” is incomplete; top scores often **1–6%** reported utilization (FICO guidance he cites).
- Paradox: all cards reporting **$0** can drop score ~15–20 pts vs small reported balances — “credit report measures how you pay debt, not only how you eliminate it.”
- His practical target: **under 10%** / single digits when realistic.
- High utilization → score drop → worse approvals → higher APR → limit cuts/closures → feedback loop.

## 2. Strategies taught

### 2.1 Shift personal card debt → business credit (headline move)
- Many **business cards do not report balances to personal** consumer reports.
- Debt still exists, but personal utilization can fall fast after transfer/spend migration.
- Often paired with **0% APR BT / purchase intros** (12–15+ months) to stop interest bleed.
- Anyone can form a sole prop / single-member LLC to access business credit (points to his separate setup video).
- Outcome he emphasizes: score becomes an asset again; unlocks better cards/limits/funding.

### 2.2 If not ready for business credit — redistribute personal balances
- FICO: **overall** utilization **and** per-card utilization.
- One maxed $5k card hurts more than three ~$1.6k cards with same total debt.
- Balance transfers can spread load; typical ~**3%** BT fee — compare to interest saved (credit unions sometimes waive).

### 2.3 Credit limit increases
- Same balances + higher limits = lower utilization.
- Works better when profile isn’t already maxed across the board.
- Many CLI requests are soft-pull — ask; don’t assume no.

### 2.4 Velocity banking (statement-date timing)
- Issuers usually report **statement balance**, not daily balance.
- Pay income onto card before cycle closes → low reported utilization → then reuse card → repeat.
- Optimizes **how debt is seen** for approvals while longer-term payoff continues.
- Can support CLI / BT / business-card approvals.

### 2.5 Revolve → installment
- Personal/consolidation loan pays cards → revolving utilization collapses.
- Installment doesn’t use revolving utilization metric (affects DTI, not the same FICO utilization bucket).
- Often lower APR than cards; more principal progress.

### 2.6 Authorized user / paid tradelines
- Trusted family AU: available credit, utilization, history boost.
- Paid tradelines: short reporting window (often 1–2 months) but history can linger; timing tool before an application (Trade Line Supply Co. video referenced).

### 2.7 Business cards with 0% BT (examples as of recording — verify live offers)
He lists (links in description; always re-check terms):
- PNC Visa Business — intro 0% purchases + BT (BT window often first 90 days) ~13 cycles
- State Farm Business Cash Rewards — 0% ~12 cycles purchases + BT
- US Bank Business Platinum — 0% ~12 cycles purchases + BT
- First Citizens (branch visit): Rewards / Cash Rewards / Low Interest business cards with 9–12 month 0% BT variants

## 4. MANUAL checklist

### Diagnose
- [ ] In product: confirm utilization is a primary score drag vs derogs.
- [ ] **MANUAL** List each card: limit, balance, cycle date, issuer, personal vs business reporting.
- [ ] **MANUAL** Note per-card maxed accounts (individual utilization killers).

### Structure moves (pick what fits qualification/risk)
- [ ] **MANUAL** Business credit path: entity readiness, applications, 0% BT offers, confirm **personal reporting** behavior of each card.
- [ ] **MANUAL** Or redistribute via personal BT / same-bank limit transplant (see overnight playbook).
- [ ] **MANUAL** Or CLI requests (prefer soft-pull).
- [ ] **MANUAL** Or installment consolidation if DTI/cash-flow supports.
- [ ] **MANUAL** Velocity banking: map statement dates; schedule paydowns before close; avoid interest/late by still paying due dates.
- [ ] **MANUAL** AU/tradeline only with eyes open on temporary/lender scrutiny.

### After moves
- [ ] **MANUAL** Wait for statement + bureau updates; re-pull reports.
- [ ] In product: re-analyze; update Credit Intelligence / progress PDFs for client.
- [ ] **MANUAL** Keep paying; 0% windows end — have payoff plan.

### Product does not
1. Apply for business/personal cards or loans.
2. Know live 0% offer terms (they change — always verify).
3. Guarantee cards won’t report to personal files (issuer-specific).
4. Automate velocity banking.
5. Replace debt counseling for overextended clients.
